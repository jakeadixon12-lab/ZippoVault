Collector Fundamentals: How to Grade & Inspect

Pocket lighter value is determined by four pillars: Authenticity, Box Completeness, Date Verification, and Surface Wear.

Mint in Box (MIB)

Unfired, flint tube unstuck, original orange safety seal intact, matching sleeve, and paperwork. Commands 100% of fair secondary comps.

Excellent / Light User

Fired occasionally, clean wick chimney with light carbon, tight hinge welds. Typically 60%–75% of MIB valuation.

What to Check: Hinge & Cam

Inspect the barrel hinge. Factory welds should show clean circular spot marks without seam cracks. Loose "wobble" indicates pocket wear.

Red Flags: Restamps & Fakes

Watch for uneven laser-etched bottom stamps on pre-2000 models (originals were roll-stamped) or blurred Bradford PA lettering.

Quick Era Stamp Cheat Sheet

• 2001–Present: Letter (A–L) + 2-digit Year (e.g. A 23)
• 1986–2000: Letter + Roman Numerals (e.g. G XII)
• 1966–1985: Vertical Slashes ( | / \ )
• 1958–1965: Dots flanking logo ( ••• ••• )
